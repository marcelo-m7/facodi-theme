# FACODI product context

FACODI is a public education and community website with standard Odoo eLearning
surfaces. Students discover courses and follow learning paths; curators organize
learning resources; contributors and partners find ways to participate.

Preserve the committed real FACODI identity: Ink #142846, Cyan #37BED2,
Blue #3979C8, Mint #A7E8BE, Sun #EFFF00 and Paper #F9FAFB; geometric cards,
strong borders, offset shadows, and existing typography with local fallbacks.
No new visual identity or font downloads are required.

Website Builder owns editorial content, menus and color combinations. eLearning
owns courses and learner behavior. This theme supplies reusable presentation,
accessible keyboard focus and responsive layouts without parallel business logic.
